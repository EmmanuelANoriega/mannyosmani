## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Technical Support Engineer and Application Administrator** with **10+ years of experience** supporting enterprise technology, mission-critical applications, and global customers across **FinTech, Healthcare, SaaS, Cloud, and Enterprise IT environments**.

My professional experience spans **Tier 2/3 technical support, application administration, identity and access management, enterprise mobility, networking, real-time communications, and complex troubleshooting**.
